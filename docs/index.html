How to subscribe

iPhone / iPad / Mac → tap “Subscribe” → Add. (Mac: File ▸ New Calendar Subscription, paste the URL.)

Google Calendar (desktop) → Other calendars ▸ + ▸ From URL → paste the feed URL → Add. Then it syncs to the app on Android/iPhone.

Outlook → Add calendar ▸ Subscribe from web → paste the URL.

Notes

  • ITV = ITV1 / ITVX (STV in Scotland) | BBC = BBC TV / iPlayer. Wales games are also on S4C, in Welsh.
  • Games without a home nation aren't on free UK TV (they're Prime Video pay-per-view), so they're not in this calendar.
  • Knockouts: a home nation's quarter-final or promotion/relegation play-off (March 2027) and Finals games (June 2027) appear as soon as the tie is set. The final itself goes in once a free UK broadcaster is confirmed (ITV showed it in 2025).
  • ⏱️ Kickoffs are stored in UTC and shown in your device's own time zone.
